Chronology of the Sanskrit corpus

Every datable text in the Параллельный санскритско-русский корпус and the Sanskrit–English corpus, placed on a timeline. Dates are taken from the VisualDCS chronology (not re-derived): a text either carries its own DCS date, inherits its period bucket's date, or — for author-datable medieval works — a flagged scholarly date. Classification scheme after DharmaMitra sanskrit-dating.
